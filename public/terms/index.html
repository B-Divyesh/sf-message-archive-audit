Terms

Archive Audit is a local inspection aid. It inventories the files you select and creates a record of what it could read; it does not certify an export, recover unavailable messages, access providers, or prove that unselected data never existed.

You are responsible for preserving the source export and reviewing any missing-reference findings. Standard MIME EML and text MBOX are supported; encrypted and proprietary stores are not.

The optional upgrade is a one-time license sold by Sociobot, with Dodo as merchant of record. A refunded or revoked license may become inactive. Refund handling is through the hosted checkout provider.
