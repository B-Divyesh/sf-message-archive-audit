Use Archive Audit as a local inspection aid

What the receipt means

Archive Audit inventories only the files you select. A receipt does not certify a provider export or prove that unselected data never existed.

Keep the source export. Review every missing-reference finding before relying on a receipt.

Supported files

The parser accepts standard MIME EML and text MBOX. It does not decrypt mail, recover unavailable messages, access providers, or read proprietary message databases.

Availability

The app is provided as-is, without a promise that it fits every archive or legal requirement.
