Terms for checking an email export

What a downloaded receipt means

Archive Audit inventories only the files you select. A downloaded receipt does not certify a provider export or prove that unselected data never existed.

Keep the email export. Review every missing-file finding before relying on a downloaded receipt.

Supported files

The parser accepts EML message files and MBOX email collections. It does not decrypt mail, recover unavailable messages, access providers, or read proprietary message databases.

Availability

The app is provided as-is. It may not fit every archive or legal requirement.

Check email exports before account or device access ends.
