Files and reports

Archive Audit reads files you choose inside your browser. It does not upload messages, attachment bytes, hashes, or receipts.

A real audit stores its latest report in IndexedDB until you clear it. The report contains message headers, filenames, sizes, and hashes. It does not contain source message bodies or attachment bytes.

Demo reports stay in memory and are discarded when you leave or reload the demo.

Network and downloads

The app makes no analytics, advertising, account, or payment requests. Exported receipts download directly to your device.

Control your data

Use “Clear local report” to remove a saved report. You can also clear this site’s storage in your browser settings.
