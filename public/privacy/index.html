Privacy for your email export

Files and summaries

Archive Audit reads files you choose inside your browser. It does not upload messages, attachment bytes, file hashes, or downloaded receipts.

A real audit stores its latest local audit summary in browser storage (IndexedDB) until you clear it. The summary contains message headers, filenames, sizes, and file hashes. It does not contain source message bodies or attachment bytes.

Demo audit summaries stay in memory and are discarded when you leave or reload the demo.

Network and downloads

The app makes no analytics, advertising, account, or payment requests. Downloaded receipts save directly to your device.

Control your data

Use “Clear local report” to remove a saved summary. You can also clear this site’s storage in your browser settings.

Check email exports before account or device access ends.
