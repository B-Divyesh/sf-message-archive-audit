Privacy

Archive Audit parses selected files in your browser. It does not upload your messages, attachment bytes, hashes, or receipts to us.

A compact audit report may be saved in your browser’s IndexedDB so it survives a refresh. You can clear it from the report. Exported files are downloaded directly to your device.

If you use the optional purchase unlock, the license token is stored in localStorage and sent only to Sociobot’s license-verification endpoint. Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout as merchant of record. We do not use advertising analytics or third-party fonts.
